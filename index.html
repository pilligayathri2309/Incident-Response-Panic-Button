Frequently asked questions

Simple answers for teams under pressure.

Who can trigger the panic button?

Authorized responders, security leads, and designated on-call operators can activate it according to incident policy.

What happens when it is activated?

It initiates a predefined escalation sequence, notifies stakeholders, and starts incident logging and triage workflow.

Can it be used for non-cyber incidents?

Yes. It supports major outages, critical service failures, and other operational emergencies that require immediate coordination.

How is this different from a normal alert?

A panic button is a deliberate emergency escalation designed to prioritize speed, ownership, and urgent action.
